# 18th Avenue, Bandar Seri Begawan: pitch site

A concept revamp of [18thavenue.com.bn](https://18thavenue.com.bn). The home page follows the design, section order and
motion of [belgradearbor.rs](https://belgradearbor.rs/en) (an Awwwards Site of the Day): full-bleed film, a pinned
introduction with image panes flying through 3D space, giant serif capitals, split-screen pickers, a view from above
and a sky-gradient footer. Every project fact, image and film clip is the developer's own.

**Stack:** Next 16.4 (App Router, Turbopack, Cache Components), React 19, Tailwind 4, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText),
Lenis, three.js with React Three Fiber and drei (the 3D page and the Residences picker), lucide-react.

**Type:** Instrument Serif and Work Sans. Belgrade Arbor uses Editorial Old (a paid font) with Work Sans; Instrument
Serif is the closest free match to its narrow text and calligraphic italic. To return to 18th Avenue's own typefaces
(Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat), change the two loaders in `src/app/layout.tsx`.

**Palette** (from the reference's CSS): vanilla `#fdfbef`, pine `#2e3a26`, navy `#314759`, lime `#e7fd55`,
golden `#a68150`, cream `#fff4d6`, cream-dark `#ccbf99`. Tokens are in `src/app/globals.css`.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # http://localhost:3230
npm run build
npm run lint
npm run typecheck
```

## Pages

**`/` (Home)**, in the reference's order:
1. Intro curtain: the five towers sketched in pencil lines, then the film settles in.
2. **Hero:** the towers on film, edge to edge, with "18TH AVENUE" set across the full width.
3. **Introduction** (pinned): three chapters on pine green. Image panes fly through 3D space, the copy trades sides, and the tagline closes in capitals. A hairline counts 01/02/03.
4. **Location** (pinned): the aerial film opens from a framed pane to full bleed, the paragraph surfaces word by word, and LOCATION condenses out of a blur.
5. **What's around:** a serif list beside a tilted 3D OpenStreetMap; picking a place draws the drive there.
6. **Residences:** golden capitals on cream silk, then a split screen of the five tower types and the show-flat rooms.
7. **Shop, dine, play & live:** the shop street on film.
8. **Five towers:** the tower list, homes per tower and a live key plan of the real wings.
   - **Tower anatomy** (pinned, after [stillform.homes](https://stillform.homes)): an "18TH AVENUE" marquee runs behind
     a small film still, which opens to full screen and dissolves like ink into a live 3D Tower 1. Seven chapters
     (Full View, Flat Slabs, Shear Walls, Private Lift, Two Homes a Floor, Façade & Glass, Basement) each keep one
     layer solid and turn the rest into ghosted navy line drawing. A rail and a progress bar count 01–07, and you can
     drag to turn the tower. An end card leads on to `/experience`. Code: `src/components/home/TowerAnatomy.tsx`,
     model and scene in `src/three/anatomy/`.
9. **Bungalows in the air** (navy): the development's signature line across the full width.
10. **Amenities:** the pool on film, then the facilities as a split screen.
11. **View from above** (pinned): a pan across the client's site plan as cloud banks part.
12. **The development:** the key figures, counting up.
13. Footer: "Join the world of 18th Avenue" register form, nav, disclaimer, and the towers standing in the sky.

**`/experience`** (3D Experience): the live WebGL site model, as before. Bungalows in the air (Tower 1 opens up, a floor
built from the traced Type 1 walls, the lift down to the basement), the seven-chapter explorer, the light study (the
real Bandar Seri Begawan sun path) and the finish studio.

**`/residences`**: all 13 layouts with the 3D tower and floor picker, self-drawing floor plans, key plans, a compare
tray and deep links (`?tower=3&layout=t3`). "Enquire" fills the footer form.

**`/about`**: the live About copy as a scroll story.

**How the 3D layout was established:**
- **Plot location:** the active construction site on the east side of the southern end of Jalan Haji Basir, found on satellite imagery. Its size (2.6 acres measured against 2.5 published) and skew match the drawing.
- **Orientation:** the drawing's "up" is north (Jalan Haji Basir runs at a bearing of 3°).
- **Sun:** NOAA solar position for 4.9° N, on a fixed reference day (10 November) so server and client renders agree.

## Discrepancies on the live site (reported, not invented around)

- **Total homes:** the unit table sums to **158** homes; the headline says **159**. The site shows 159 and notes the table total on Residences.
- **Size range:** "Condo sizes varies between 1554.8 ft² to 1950.4 ft²", but the table's smallest unit is 1,184.0. The site uses **1,184–1,950 sq ft**.
- **Tower 5** runs to floor 16 (2nd–16th), not 18. **Tower 4** has one home per floor.
- **"+1" in the bedroom counts** (3BR+1, 2BR+1) only appears on layouts with a utility room, so it is shown as "+ 1", not as a study.
- **HTML glitches fixed:** "Ultilities" becomes Utility, and Tower 1's "123/td>" becomes 123. Tower 5 Ground A repeats an area block; 1,813.7 is used.
- **About counters:** the live "missions" counters all read 0, so they were dropped.
- **Logos:** `logo_dark.png` is the web agency's (Biz Digital) logo; the 18th Avenue wordmark was redrawn as SVG.
- **Interior caption:** the second interior popup labelled "BATH" shows the kitchen island (its file is `VIEW-TO-KITCHEN`), so it is captioned as such.

## Interpretations and wording to confirm with the client

**Home page wording that is ours** (everything else in `src/content/home.ts` is live or condensed from live copy)
- Kickers: "18th Avenue design", "In the heart of the capital", "What's around".
- Section labels: "Five towers", "The development", "Register your interest and the sales team will be in touch."
- The amenity detail lines under each live tagline, and "A refreshing swim in our lagoon pool." (adapted from the film).
- "Shop, dine, play & live" is the film's closing line ("Shop, dine, play and live at 18th Avenue").
- Tower anatomy: the "Tower anatomy" label, the chapter names, the end card ("One tower, layer by layer / Now explore
  all of 18th Avenue") and the lift tags. Each chapter's one-line stat is condensed from the live "Features of
  Development" and "Our Attractions".
- Tower anatomy model: the shear-wall layout (cross walls through each wing) is a diagram, not the structural drawings.

**Imagery**
- The loops and most stills are frames from the developer's own film (artist's impressions), cropped to remove its watermark and subtitles.
- The silk and cloud backdrops are generated, not photographs.

**Travel times**
- OSRM estimates on OpenStreetMap roads, free-flow (no traffic).
- Crown Park shows the developer's own "5 minutes walk".
- Two coordinates were placed by hand: the Waterfront promenade, and the golf course below the airport (the client's map calls it RBA Golf Course).

**3D page and Residences** (unchanged from the first version)
- Shops modelled under the tennis court, after the client's perspective and the drawing's "I. Driveway Entry to Shops". Not confirmed.
- Explorer chapter titles, the bungalow steps and the finish names are our wording; the finishes are not a specification.
- Glass, white slab edges, bronze corner panels and roof crowns, after the client's renders and film; 3.3 m floor-to-floor; OpenStreetMap neighbourhood massing.

**Forms**
- The register form (footer, every page) opens the visitor's email app (`mailto:`). There is no backend.

## Notes for developers

- **Motion:** GSAP timelines scrubbed by ScrollTrigger over CSS-sticky frames (no pin spacers), smoothed by Lenis. Text is split on the server (`src/components/type/Split.tsx`) so the HTML matches what animates; an inline script adds `html.js` before first paint so hidden reveal states never flash. Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Videos** (`src/components/ui/Video.tsx`) load and play only near the viewport; the hero loads at once.
- **`is-loaded`** on `<html>` releases "on load" reveals: the intro curtain sets it on Home, `SmoothScroll` on every other page.
- The 3D page uses one fixed canvas (`src/components/stage/SiteStage.tsx`); sections own the shot through `useStageSection`. Software renderers get the light pipeline (no AO or bloom).
- `src/components/legacy/` holds the earlier home sections; nothing imports them.
- **Not yet verified:** real-GPU frame rates and iOS Safari. Testing was headless Chromium (software WebGL) at 1600×900, 1903×870 and 390×844.
